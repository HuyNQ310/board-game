Exploding Kittens Online Rules & 12-Card Guide

Mèo Nổ Online (Exploding Kittens Online) is a free real-time multiplayer survival card game for 2–10 players directly in your browser. Create or join a room with a 5-character code—no app install or mandatory login required—and enjoy 12 strategic cards, a zero-leak 5-second Nope window, and built-in WebRTC voice chat.

1. Core Rules & Turn Flow

Starting Setup

Every player is dealt 5 cards: 1 Defuse (Kìm Cắt Dây) and 4 ordinary cards. The draw pile contains players - 1 Exploding Kittens, and scales automatically for 7–10 player lobbies.

30-Second Turn Timer

On your turn, play 0 or more action cards or cat pairs, then draw 1 card to end your turn. If the 30-second timer expires, the server automatically draws or defuses to the middle of the deck.

Zero-Leak 5s Nope Window

Playing an action card or Cat Pair triggers a 5-second Nope window. Any eligible opponent can play Nope (KHÔNG!) to cancel it—or stack another Nope to counter-cancel. Hands and deck order never leak to other players.

Reconnect Grace & Winning

Disconnected players keep their seat for 120 seconds so they can rejoin seamlessly. The last surviving player wins the match!

4. Frequently Asked Questions

How many cards does each player start with?

5 cards: 1 Defuse (Kìm Cắt Dây) plus 4 random ordinary cards.

Can I pair two different cat types together?

No. A Cat Pair requires 2 identical cats (two Striped Cats, two Taco Cats, or two Hairy Potato Cats).

Do I need to install an app or register?

No download or login is required. Everything runs in modern mobile and desktop web browsers.
